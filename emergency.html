STAY CALM & ACT SAFELY

What Should You Do?

Follow these steps if something happens online.

STEP 1

Stay Calm

Do not panic or respond aggressively.

STEP 2

Save Evidence

Take screenshots and keep messages, usernames and links.

STEP 3

Block & Report

Block the account and report it through the platform.

STEP 4

Tell a Trusted Adult

Speak with your counsellor, principal, teacher or parent.

STEP 5

Use CyberSafe

Submit a confidential cyberbullying report through CyberSafe.
