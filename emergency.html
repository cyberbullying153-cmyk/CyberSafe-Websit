What Should You Do?

Follow these steps if something happens online.

1

Stay calm

Do not panic or respond aggressively.

2

Save evidence

Take screenshots and keep messages, usernames and links.

3

Block and report

Block the person and report the account on the platform.

4

Tell a trusted adult

Speak with your counsellor, principal, teacher or parent.

5

Use CyberSafe

Submit a confidential cyberbullying report through the CyberSafe portal.

❤️

Remember

Cyberbullying is not your fault. You do not have to deal with it alone. Reach out to someone you trust and get help as soon as possible.
