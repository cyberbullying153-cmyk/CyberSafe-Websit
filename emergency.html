🛡️ What Should You Do?

  • 🛑 Do not reply to abusive or threatening messages.
  • 📸 Save screenshots and other evidence.
  • 🚫 Block and report the account responsible.
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Tell a trusted adult or teacher.
  • 📝 Submit a CyberSafe report so your school can help.
  • 🚨 If there is an immediate danger, contact 112.
