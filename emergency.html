Emergency Help

If you are in immediate danger or facing serious online harassment, contact a trusted adult, your school counsellor, or the appropriate authorities.

School Contacts

  • 👨‍🏫 School Counsellor
  • 👩‍💼 Principal
  • 💻 IT Department
  • 📞 Class Teacher

Emergency Steps

  1. Do not respond to abusive messages.
  2. Take screenshots as evidence.
  3. Block and report the account.
  4. Tell a trusted adult or teacher.
  5. Submit a report using the CyberSafe portal.
