Emergency Help

If you are facing cyberbullying, online threats, harassment, hacking, or any serious online problem, please contact a trusted adult or the appropriate support service.

What is CyberSafe?

CyberSafe is a student security platform designed to help students stay safe online, report cyberbullying, learn about digital safety, and connect with trusted school staff.

Students can use CyberSafe to report incidents safely and confidentially and find important support resources.

What Should You Do?

1

Do not reply to abusive or threatening messages.

2

Take screenshots and save them as evidence.

3

Block and report the abusive account.

4

Tell a trusted adult, teacher, counsellor, or parent.

5

Submit a report through CyberSafe.

6

For serious cybercrime, contact the Government Cyber Crime Helpline at 1930.
