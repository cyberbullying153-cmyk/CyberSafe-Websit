🚨 Emergency Help

If you are in immediate danger or facing serious online harassment, contact a trusted adult, your school counsellor, or the appropriate authorities.

👨‍🏫 School Counsellor

Talk to your school counsellor for emotional support and guidance.

👩‍💼 Principal

Report serious cyberbullying incidents to your school administration.

💻 IT Department

Get help with hacked accounts, fake profiles, or technical issues.

📞 Class Teacher

Your class teacher can help you report and resolve problems safely.

What Should You Do?

1️⃣ Do not reply to abusive messages.
2️⃣ Take screenshots as evidence.
3️⃣ Block and report the account.
4️⃣ Tell a trusted adult or teacher.
5️⃣ Submit a report through CyberSafe.
