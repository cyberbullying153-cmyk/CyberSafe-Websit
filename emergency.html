If You Are In Immediate Danger

Move to a safe place and tell a trusted adult, parent, teacher or school staff member immediately. Do not meet an online stranger alone and do not send money or personal information to someone threatening you.

🧭

STAY SAFE

What Should You Do?

Follow these simple steps if something happens online.

1

Stay Calm

Do not panic or respond aggressively to the person.

2

Save Evidence

Take screenshots and keep messages, usernames, links and other evidence.

3

Block & Report

Block the person and report the account on the platform.

4

Tell a Trusted Adult

Speak with your counsellor, principal, teacher or parent.

5

Use CyberSafe

Submit a confidential cyberbullying report through CyberSafe.
