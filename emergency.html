What Should You Do?

1

Do not respond

Do not argue with or reply to abusive messages.

2

Save evidence

Take screenshots and keep messages as evidence.

3

Block and report

Block the person and report the account on the platform.

4

Tell a trusted adult

Speak with your parent, teacher, counsellor, or principal.

5

Report through CyberSafe

Submit a confidential report through the CyberSafe portal.

6

Get official help

For serious cybercrime, contact the official cybercrime service.

Need to report an incident?

You can submit a confidential CyberSafe report to your school.
