🆘 Need Help Right Now?

If someone is threatening, harassing, bullying, or making you feel unsafe online, you do not have to handle it alone.

  • 👨‍🏫 Talk to a trusted teacher
  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Tell a parent or trusted adult
  • 📸 Save screenshots and evidence
  • 🚫 Block and report the account
Open Emergency Help →

🔐 Remember: Protect Yourself Online

Never share your password, OTP, personal information, or private photos with strangers. If something online makes you uncomfortable, save the evidence and tell a trusted adult or teacher.
