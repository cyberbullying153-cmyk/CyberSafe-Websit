Learn About Cyberbullying

Understand what cyberbullying is, recognize warning signs, and know how to respond safely.

📱 What is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is the use of phones, social media, gaming platforms, or messaging apps to harass, threaten, or embarrass someone.

⚠ Common Types

  • Online harassment
  • Fake accounts
  • Sharing private photos
  • Spreading rumors
  • Excluding someone online

😔 Warning Signs

  • Avoiding phone or computer
  • Sudden sadness
  • Loss of confidence
  • Fear of going to school

🛡 What You Should Do

  • Save evidence.
  • Block the bully.
  • Report the account.
  • Tell a trusted adult.
  • Never retaliate.
