What is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is bullying that happens through digital devices such as mobile phones, computers, gaming platforms, messaging applications, and social media.

It can include sending threatening or hurtful messages, spreading rumors, sharing embarrassing information, posting harmful content, or repeatedly targeting someone online.

CyberSafe helps students report incidents safely, learn about digital safety, and connect with trusted teachers and school support staff.
