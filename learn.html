What is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is the repeated use of phones, social media, gaming platforms, or messaging apps to harass, threaten, embarrass, or harm someone online.

📱

Harassment

Repeated hurtful messages and comments.

🎭

Fake Accounts

Impersonating someone to cause harm.

📢

Rumours

Spreading false information online.

📷

Private Photos

Sharing personal images without permission.

Warning Signs

😟 Sudden sadness after using a phone
📵 Avoiding social media
😴 Poor sleep
📚 Loss of concentration
😨 Fear of going to school
💬 Becoming unusually quiet

How to Respond

1. Stay Calm

Don't reply to abusive messages.

2. Save Evidence

Take screenshots before deleting anything.

3. Block & Report

Report harmful accounts immediately.

4. Tell a Trusted Adult

Speak with a parent, teacher, or counsellor.

5. Get Official Help

Use the CyberSafe Emergency page if needed.

Your Mental Health Matters ❤️

Cyberbullying can affect confidence, sleep, friendships, and studies. Asking for help is a sign of strength—not weakness.
