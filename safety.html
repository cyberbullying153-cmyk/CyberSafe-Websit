🛡️ CYBERSAFE PROTECTION

Online Safety Tips

Simple steps to protect yourself, your accounts and your personal information online.

🔐 STAY PROTECTED

Smart Habits for a Safer Internet

Follow these practical tips whenever you use social media, messaging apps, games or other online services.

🔐
01

Use Strong Passwords

Create strong and unique passwords for your accounts. Avoid using your name, birthday or easy-to-guess information.

🔢
02

Never Share OTPs or Passwords

Never share your password, OTP, PIN or verification code with anyone, even if they claim to be a friend, teacher or company.

📸
03

Save Screenshots as Evidence

If someone sends abusive, threatening or inappropriate messages, save screenshots and keep the evidence safe.

🚫
04

Block and Report Abusive Users

Do not continue arguing with someone who is harassing you. Block the account and report it through the platform.

👨‍👩‍👧
05

Tell a Trusted Adult

If something online makes you feel unsafe, uncomfortable or threatened, tell a parent, teacher, counsellor or another trusted adult immediately.

🌐
06

Think Before Posting

Remember that things posted online can be copied and shared. Think carefully before posting photos, comments or personal information.

🛡️
🚨 IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

You Are Not Alone

If you experience cyberbullying, threats, harassment or anything that makes you feel unsafe, don't deal with it alone.

🚨 Report Cyberbullying 📞 Get Help
