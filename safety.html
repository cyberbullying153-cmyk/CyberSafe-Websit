ONLINE SAFETY GUIDE

Protect Yourself Online

Follow these simple habits every day to make your online experience safer.

01
🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Protect your accounts with passwords that are difficult for others to guess.

  • Use long and unique passwords.
  • Do not reuse the same password everywhere.
  • Use a trusted password manager if possible.
02
🔑

Protect Your Passwords & OTPs

Never give your password, OTP or verification code to another person.

  • Never share OTPs.
  • Never share login codes.
  • Be careful with suspicious requests.
03
📸

Save Evidence

If cyberbullying happens, keep useful evidence before deleting anything.

  • Take screenshots.
  • Keep usernames and profile links.
  • Save important messages.
04
🚫

Block & Report

You do not have to continue communicating with someone who is abusing or threatening you.

  • Block abusive accounts.
  • Report harmful content.
  • Do not respond aggressively.
05
👨‍👩‍👧

Tell a Trusted Adult

You should never deal with serious online problems completely alone.

  • Talk to your parent or guardian.
  • Speak with your teacher.
  • Contact your school counsellor.
06
🌐

Think Before You Post

Remember that things shared online can be copied, saved or forwarded.

  • Think before posting.
  • Protect personal information.
  • Check who can see your content.
07
👤

Protect Personal Information

Keep sensitive information private and only share it with people you trust.

  • Do not publicly share your address.
  • Be careful sharing your phone number.
  • Protect school and family information.
08
🎣

Watch Out for Scams

Some messages are designed to trick you into giving away information or money.

  • Do not click suspicious links.
  • Check unexpected messages carefully.
  • Ask a trusted adult if unsure.
09
📱

Keep Your Devices Secure

Keep your phone, computer and apps protected with basic security measures.

  • Use a screen lock.
  • Keep software updated.
  • Only install trusted apps.

🛡️ Remember These Rules

When something online feels wrong, stop, save evidence and ask for help.

❌
Never share OTPs Legitimate people should not ask you for your private verification codes.
🔒
Keep accounts private Review your privacy and account settings regularly.
📸
Save evidence Keep screenshots and important information if an incident occurs.
🤝
Ask for help Talking to a trusted adult is a strength, not a weakness.

🚨 Something serious happened?

If you are facing threats, serious harassment, blackmail or another urgent situation, get help from a trusted adult immediately.
