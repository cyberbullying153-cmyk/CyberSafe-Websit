ONLINE SAFETY

Stay Safe. Stay CyberSafe.

Follow these essential tips to protect yourself from cyberbullying and online threats.

1
🔐

Use Strong Passwords

Create unique passwords with letters, numbers and symbols.

2
🚫

Never Share OTPs

Keep OTPs, passwords and personal information private.

3
📱

Think Before You Post

Anything posted online can stay online forever.

4
🚨

Report Bullying

Report harmful behaviour immediately instead of responding.

5
📸

Save Evidence

Take screenshots before deleting messages.

6
👨‍🏫

Tell a Trusted Adult

Talk to a teacher, parent or guardian whenever you feel unsafe.
