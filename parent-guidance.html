👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Guidance

Helping parents protect children from cyberbullying and promote safe online behaviour.

🔍 Warning Signs

  • Sudden mood changes after using a phone or computer.
  • Avoiding school or friends.
  • Deleting social media accounts unexpectedly.
  • Becoming anxious while receiving messages.
  • Loss of confidence or self-esteem.

💬 Talk to Your Child

  • Stay calm and listen carefully.
  • Avoid blaming or judging.
  • Encourage honest conversations.
  • Reassure them they are not alone.
  • Support them emotionally.

🛡 Online Safety Tips

  • Use strong passwords.
  • Enable Two-Factor Authentication.
  • Keep social media accounts private.
  • Never share OTPs or passwords.
  • Teach children not to talk to strangers online.

📱 Social Media Rules

  • Review privacy settings together.
  • Know which apps your child uses.
  • Limit screen time.
  • Follow age restrictions.
  • Report abusive users immediately.

🚨 If Cyberbullying Happens

  • Stay calm.
  • Save screenshots as evidence.
  • Block the bully.
  • Report the account.
  • Inform the school.
  • Contact local authorities if threats are involved.

❤️ How Parents Can Help

  • Spend quality time with your child.
  • Encourage positive friendships.
  • Monitor online activities responsibly.
  • Promote respectful online behaviour.
  • Seek counselling if needed.

💡 Parent's Daily Checklist

✅ Ask your child how their day online was.

✅ Check privacy settings monthly.

✅ Encourage reporting of cyberbullying immediately.

✅ Keep communication open and supportive.

✅ Be a positive digital role model.
