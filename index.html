What is CyberSafe?

CyberSafe is a student security portal designed to help students stay safe online. Students can report cyberbullying confidentially, learn about digital safety, access helpful resources, and connect with trusted teachers and adults.

Our goal is simple: No student should feel unsafe, alone, or afraid because of something happening online.
