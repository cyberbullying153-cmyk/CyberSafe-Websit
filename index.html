What is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is bullying through digital devices such as mobile phones, computers, gaming platforms, and social media. It includes spreading rumours, sending threatening messages, sharing private images without permission, impersonating someone, or repeatedly harassing them online.

CyberSafe empowers students to report incidents anonymously, learn about digital safety, and connect with trusted teachers and counsellors for help.
