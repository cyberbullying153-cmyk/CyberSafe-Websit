📚 Learn About Cyberbullying

What is Cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is bullying through social media, messaging apps, gaming platforms, or other online spaces.

Common Types

Harassment, fake profiles, spreading rumours, threats, impersonation, and sharing private photos.

Warning Signs

Fear of using devices, sadness, anxiety, avoiding school, or sudden withdrawal from friends.

How to Respond

Stay calm, save evidence, block the offender, report the account, and tell a trusted adult.
